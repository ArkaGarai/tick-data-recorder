Privacy & Google authorization

Privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This policy describes Tick Data Recorder, a personal desktop market-data recording and archival project maintained by Arka Garai, and its Google Drive integration through rclone. It also describes this public project website.

Information processed and why

  • Market-data recordings: stock-market observations, compressed archives, file metadata, and catalog information are processed to record, organize, verify, store, and retrieve research data.
  • Google Drive data: when an account owner authorizes the integration, the desktop workflow can access Drive files and metadata as permitted by the granted scope. Its intended use is uploading, listing, verifying, downloading, and managing the project’s archive files.
  • Authorization information: rclone uses Google-issued OAuth tokens to carry out the authorized Drive operations. Google account passwords are not requested by Tick Data Recorder; sign-in happens with Google.

Scope of Google Drive access

The configured Google Drive scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. This is a broad permission that can permit viewing, creating, modifying, and deleting Drive files, including files outside the project’s archives. The intended project use is archive management. Use a dedicated archive account if you want to keep unrelated files separate.

Where data is stored

Recordings, catalogs, configuration, and authorization tokens are held in the operator’s local environment. Archive files are transferred to the Google Drive accounts authorized for the workflow. This static project website does not receive OAuth tokens or archive uploads and does not provide a sign-in form.

Sharing and use restrictions

Project data is transferred to Google Drive only as part of the owner-authorized archival workflow. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred to data brokers. It is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Any use of Google API data is limited to providing and improving the user-facing archival functions described here, subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

rclone performs authorized transfers on the operator’s computer. Google processes data stored in Google Drive under its own policies. No separate hosted Tick Data Recorder service receives Google user data.

Retention and deletion

The operator controls local and Drive archive retention. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing local files or Drive archives. To delete data, remove the relevant local files and the corresponding files in Google Drive, including Drive Trash where appropriate. Remove the local rclone configuration or stored tokens when retiring an account connection.

Revoking access

You can revoke Tick Data Recorder’s access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. You can also disconnect the rclone remote locally. Previously stored copies remain until you delete them.

Security

Keep OAuth tokens, client secrets, and local configuration private. Do not upload them to this public website repository or include them in support requests. Access to the computer and authorized Google accounts should be restricted to their owner.

This website

This website uses no project-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard request information under GitHub’s privacy practices. A support issue you choose to submit is public; include only information you are comfortable publishing.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact the maintainer through the project’s GitHub issues. Do not include private account information. This page will be updated when the described practices change, with the effective date above revised accordingly.
