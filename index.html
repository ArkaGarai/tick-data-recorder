Tick Data Recorder

A personal desktop project for recording stock-market tick observations and keeping compressed research archives.

Tick Data Recorder supports an intraday market-data research workflow: collect observations from an authorized market-data source, save them locally, compress completed recordings, and organize archives for later analysis.

Capture locally

Market observations are recorded on the operator’s computer for historical analysis.

Keep organized archives

Compressed files and a local catalog help track recordings and their integrity.

Back up with Drive

The Google Drive integration uses rclone and Google OAuth to transfer archive files to Google accounts explicitly authorized by their owner.

Why Google sign-in is used

Google authorization connects the desktop archival workflow to the operator’s Google Drive. The existing OAuth application, Tick Data Recorder, is used with the desktop client Tick Recorder Windows. Google Drive access is for archive storage and retrieval; it does not provide brokerage access or execute trades.

The configured Drive permission can allow broad access to Drive files. Authorize only an account you intend to use for this workflow, and review Google’s consent screen before granting access.

Project status and support

This is a personal research tool maintained by Arka Garai, rather than a hosted data-collection service. This public website documents the project and its privacy practices. The recording program runs separately on the operator’s computer.

For questions about the project or privacy, open an issue in the website repository. Do not post access tokens, passwords, private files, or account credentials in public issues.
